OUR STORY

A little more room
for reading.

Shelfora brings the everyday work of a library together, from finding a book and reserving it to keeping track of loans and reading-room seats.

Explore the catalogue

Made for the library community

Shelfora is a library management project for small academic and institutional libraries. Members can explore the catalogue and follow their activity, while librarians can manage records, reservations and circulation in one place.
